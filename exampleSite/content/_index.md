Welcome. This is a [digital garden](/notes/what-is-a-digital-garden/) — a collection of notes, ideas, and explorations that grow over time. Unlike blog posts ordered by dates, this is an interlinked web of living documents.

This particular garden is the demo site for **[Kavu](https://github.com/stultus/kavu)**, a Hugo theme for digital gardens. The notes you see are short examples; replace them with your own. Each note carries a maturity badge — 🌱 seedling, 🌿 growing, or 🌳 evergreen — so readers can tell at a glance whether they're reading a half-formed thought or something stable.

Start anywhere and follow the links.
