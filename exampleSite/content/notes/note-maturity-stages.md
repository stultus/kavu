Kavu uses a `status` field in note frontmatter to mark how mature each note is. The three values map to colours in the network graph and badges on each note page:

| Status | Meaning | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| `seeding` 🌱 | A stub or rough thought. Maybe a single sentence. | Amber |
| `growing` 🌿 | A developed draft. Linked to a few neighbours. Still rough at the edges. | Blue |
| `evergreen` 🌳 | Polished, well-linked, represents a stable understanding you'd point a friend at. | Green |

The system has two halves. The first half is **a low bar for creating notes** — write a [seedling](/notes/seedling-example/) the moment a thought arrives, even if it's one line. The second half is **a high bar for promoting notes** — `evergreen` means you'd actively recommend it. Most notes will sit at `growing` forever, and that's fine.

The practical effect is that you get to publish in public without performing finality. A reader can see at a glance whether they're reading a stub or a stable claim.
