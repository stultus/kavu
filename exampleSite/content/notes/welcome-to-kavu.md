This site is an example garden built with **Kavu**, a Hugo theme for digital gardens and personal knowledge bases. The notes you see are short, generic samples meant to demonstrate the theme — feel free to delete them all and start your own garden in their place.

A few things to try right now:

- Open the [What Is a Digital Garden](/notes/what-is-a-digital-garden/) note and follow the links inside it. Notice the bracketed `[[wikilink]]` rendering — that's [How Wikilinks Work](/notes/how-wikilinks-work/).
- Scroll back to the homepage and look at the **Network** graph. Each dot is a note, coloured by maturity. Hover and click around; press `/` to search.
- Check the colour-coded dots in the index. Green is evergreen, blue is growing, amber is a seedling. The system is explained in [Note Maturity Stages](/notes/note-maturity-stages/).

If you're starting fresh, the [Seedling Example](/notes/seedling-example/) shows what a half-formed thought looks like — Kavu is built around the idea that publishing rough notes is fine.
